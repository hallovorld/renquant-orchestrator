## What changes, what does not

1. **The engine's edge over the champion WIDENS net of costs**: +9.5pp
   annualized (was +6.7pp gross) and Sharpe 0.92 vs 0.49 — because the
   champion arm pays the largest cost bill. The promotion case for L2 gets
   stronger, not weaker, under costs.
2. **The floor's price persists net**: uniform beats hedge by 3.0pp (was
   2.0pp gross) — half the book chained to the costliest arm. Same trade-off,
   slightly steeper price.
3. **Cross-arm, decision-grade now**: mom_slow is the best single arm net
   (cheapest churn, highest net return); the diversified books keep the best
   drawdowns. mom_fast lands mid-pack — neither the blend-IC villain (#913)
   nor the gross-frame hero (#926 Reading 3); frame-dependence stands.
4. **Unchanged**: all of #926's assumptions 5–9 (EW within top-3, champion is
   a replay proxy, survivorship, one deterministic path); the regret theorem
   is unchanged — costs shift the sequence, not the bound's validity.
